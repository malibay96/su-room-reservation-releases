# Privacy Policy — SU Oda Özeti / SU Room Reservation

**Last updated: 5 October 2026**

SU Oda Özeti (browser extension, bookmarklet and Android app, "the Software") summarises room reservations published on Sabancı University's public SUIS room search page (suis.sabanciuniv.edu).

**Data collection:** The Software does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

**Network requests:** The Software connects only to suis.sabanciuniv.edu to read publicly available room schedules, and (Android app only) to github.com to check for a newer version. No user data is sent in these requests.

**Local storage:** Your preferences (theme, view mode, filters, selected rooms) are kept only on your device, in your browser's storage for the extension. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the extension removes them.

**Browser permissions (extension):** Host access to suis.sabanciuniv.edu is used only to fetch schedule pages. The `declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess` permission is used only to set the Origin and Referer headers on the extension's own requests to suis.sabanciuniv.edu, because the university server rejects requests from other origins. The rule applies only to requests made by this extension, never to other websites.

**Third parties:** No data is shared with third parties. The Software is not an official Sabancı University product.

**Contact:** https://github.com/malibay96/su-room-reservation-releases/issues

## Gizlilik Politikası (Türkçe özet)

SU Oda Özeti hiçbir kişisel veri toplamaz, saklamaz, iletmez veya satmaz. Yalnızca suis.sabanciuniv.edu adresindeki herkese açık oda programlarını okur; Android uygulaması ayrıca yeni sürüm kontrolü için github.com'a bağlanır. Tema ve filtre tercihleri yalnızca cihazınızda tutulur. Uzantının `declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess` izni, yalnızca kendi SUIS isteklerine Origin/Referer başlığı eklemek için kullanılır; başka sitelere uygulanmaz. Yazılım Sabancı Üniversitesi'nin resmî ürünü değildir.
